# Short-Period Ranking Design

Add three fixed lookbacks to the existing Sector ETF Leadership dashboard:

- 1 day: 1 trading day
- 1 week: 5 trading days
- 2 weeks: 10 trading days

The sidebar's ranking-basis selector and the fixed-period ranking tabs will both expose these periods. Returns continue to use adjusted closing prices and the existing full 42-ETF descending table. Existing 1, 2, 3, and 6 month periods, custom dates, chart behavior, and leadership composite remain unchanged.

Verification will confirm the new labels map to 1, 5, and 10 trading-day lookbacks and that each table still contains all 42 ETFs.
